# Anagrav — Support

Anagrav is an anagram word game for iPhone: build words from six letter tiles to fuel a rocket's climb.

**Contact:** [whatsthewordbusiness@gmail.com](mailto:whatsthewordbusiness@gmail.com)\
I read every message. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

## How to play

- On the home screen, tap **TAP TO PLAY** to take off.
- Tap letter tiles to build a word of three to six letters, then tap the empty background to submit it.
- Every accepted word refuels the rocket. Longer words give more, and words in a row build a combo multiplier.
- When the fuel runs out, the rocket falls. Find words fast to relight the engines before the fall ends the run.

## Purchases

Tile skins are one-time purchases through the App Store. Stars are earned by playing and are never sold.

**Restoring purchases** (after reinstalling, or on a new iPhone signed in to the same iCloud Apple Account):

1. Open Anagrav.
2. Tap the gear on the home screen to open Settings, and tap **RESTORE PURCHASES**.
3. Your skins return to the locker.

Restoring brings back paid skins only. Stars, scores and other progress are stored on the device and are not restored.

**Refunds** are handled by Apple: visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

**Ask to Buy:** if a purchase is waiting for a parent's approval, you can keep playing. The skin unlocks automatically once it is approved.

## Troubleshooting

- **A purchase says it's still processing:** keep playing. Approved items unlock automatically; if one hasn't appeared after a few minutes, use **Restore Purchases**.
- **"No internet connection" in the shop:** buying and restoring need a connection. The rest of the game works offline.
- **A real word was rejected:** email me the word and a case for it and I'll add it in an update.
